Product Consumption Task

    Go through the Data Marketplace
    Review the products published by other students in the MS Teams Data Marketplace folder.
    Choose one product
    Select the data product that is the most interesting or useful for you. Do not choose your own product.
    Consume the product
    Download or access the data according to the instructions provided in the product card. Load the data into Jupyter Notebook, Python, Excel, Power BI, Streamlit, or another tool of your choice.
    Create a basic visualization
    Prepare a simple chart, table, or visual summary based on the consumed data. The goal is not advanced analytics, but proving that the product can be understood and used.
    Add feedback and rating
    At the bottom of the consumed product’s 2-page document, add:
        a screenshot of your visualization,
        short feedback: what worked well, what was unclear, what could be improved,
        a rating from 1 to 5. 

Rating scale

1 — Unusable
The product cannot be used without contacting the owner. Data, access instructions, schema, or links are missing or unclear.

2 — Hard to use
The product can be used only with significant effort. Some key information is missing, the structure is unclear, or access is difficult.

3 — Usable, but needs improvement
The product can be consumed, but some parts require guessing or extra interpretation. Basic documentation exists, but it is incomplete or not fully clear.

4 — Useful and mostly clear
The product is easy to access and understand. Documentation, schema, and usage instructions are mostly complete, with only minor gaps.

5 — Very useful and easy to use
The product is well described, easy to access, and immediately usable. The schema, purpose, quality metrics, and example usage are clear enough to start working within a few minutes.

Additional points

To encourage high-quality data product publishing and meaningful feedback, additional points can be awarded:

Top 20% Most Consumed Products: +2 points

Products that are selected and consumed by the highest number of students will receive +2 bonus points.
This rewards products that are attractive, understandable, and useful for others.

High-Quality Feedback: +1 point

Students can receive +1 bonus point for providing valuable feedback on another student’s product.
High-quality feedback should be specific, constructive, and actionable.

Good feedback should answer:

    What was easy to understand?
    What was difficult or unclear?
    Was the data easy to access?
    Was the schema understandable?
    What should be improved? 

Example:

“The dataset was easy to access and the schema was mostly clear. The refresh frequency and owner were well described. However, the meaning of the status_code column was unclear, and adding example values would make the product easier to use.”
